# ENERGY BRAIN

Purpose: monitor grid, mini-grid, solar, battery, and industrial energy systems; detect unusual temporal patterns; support maintenance and resilience planning.

## Inputs

- Smart-meter data
- PMU or frequency data
- Solar inverter data
- Battery state of charge
- Transformer temperature
- Outage records
- Weather data

## Safety boundary

The Energy Brain is a monitoring and decision-support system. It must not bypass protection relays, automatically shed load, or replace qualified engineers.

## Evidence discipline

The existing TOLBIT analysis found that fast contingency and protection events, rather than slow waveform degradation, dominated the documented East African blackout cases. Therefore, do not claim that a coherence metric predicts all outages.

## First pilot

One factory, one solar mini-grid, or one distribution substation.
