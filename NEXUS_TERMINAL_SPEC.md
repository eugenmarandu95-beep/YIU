# NEXUS Terminal Specification

```bash
nexus ask "What did my mini-brain v2 establish?"
nexus audit consciousness --strict
nexus maize diagnose --photo leaf.jpg
nexus maize irrigation --soil-moisture 18 --crop maize
nexus energy monitor --source meter.csv
nexus health triage --swahili
nexus retail forecast --sales sales.csv
nexus remember "Correct: v5 did not test memory"
nexus plan "Launch maize pilot in Morogoro"
nexus execute --dry-run
```

## Requirements

- Local-first by default
- Swahili and English
- Source citations
- Approval prompts
- Offline knowledge retrieval
- Audit log
- No silent autonomous high-risk actions
