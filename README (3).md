# MAIZE BRAIN

Purpose: help farmers and agronomists understand crop state, distinguish possible causes, forecast stress, plan safe interventions, and assess market timing.

## Decision classes

- C1 Perception
- C2 Differential diagnosis
- C3 Irrigation
- C4 Planting window
- C5 Stress forecasting
- C6 Market timing
- C7 Intervention planning
- C8 Expert escalation
- C9 Feedback learning
- C10 Knowledge explanation

## Safety boundary

The Maize Brain may support observation, education, planning, and escalation. It must not autonomously diagnose disease, prescribe pesticides, or irrigate without approval.

## First pilot

One crop, one region, 20–100 farmers, agronomist-reviewed recommendations, and explicit outcome tracking.
